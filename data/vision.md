# Vision - Wonder Orchards (alpha-v2.3)

## Overview

We imagine a world where fruit trees live where people are, offering nourishment, beauty, and wonder for everyone.

Wonder Orchards exists to help make that world increasingly real.

## Core Picture

- **Community Orchards:** Transform public parks, abandoned lots, and shared spaces into orchards where anyone can enjoy the abundance.
- **Neighborhood Orchards:** Convert unused or neglected land into fruitful gathering places.
- **Household Plantings:** Place fruit trees in homes with children to nurture connection, nourishment, and joy.
- **Nurseries:** Establish nurseries to grow, propagate, and supply trees for continued orchard development.

## Guiding Spirit

- The tree is our model: patient, resilient, generous, abundant.
- Our work mirrors the seasons and cycles of growth, flowering, fruiting, and rest.
- We celebrate the wonder of fruit trees as living gifts.
- We favor abundance, participation, beauty, usefulness, and long-term care.
- We allow the work to grow organically while maintaining enough structure to make continued growth easier.

## Direction

Wonder Orchards should continually increase the presence of fruitful trees in the places where people live, gather, learn, play, and grow.

The organization may develop many forms of orchards, nurseries, educational programs, propagation systems, partnerships, and community projects over time.

The specific forms may evolve. The underlying direction remains:

**More trees. More fruit. More places to gather. More abundance available to everyone.**

## Current Objectives

1. Establish and strengthen the nonprofit organization.
2. Develop the nursery and propagation system.
3. Establish the first orchard and household planting programs.
4. Develop sustainable systems for planting, caring for, and expanding orchards.
5. Learn from each season and allow successful patterns to propagate.
