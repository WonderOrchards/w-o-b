# Workflows — Wonder Orchards

Our workflows are **modeled after the life of a tree**, reflecting patience, steady growth, and abundance.

## Tree Roadmap Stages
1. **Seeds (Vision, Planting & Cuttings)**
2. **Roots (Foundation & Values)**
3. **Trunk (Structure & Stability)**
4. **Branches (Programs & Projects)**
5. **Leaves (People & Daily Work)**
6. **Flowers (Ideas & Initiatives)**
7. **Fruit (Results & Impact)**
8. **Cycles & Seasons (Pacing & Patience)**

## Active Task Workflow
- Tasks tracked in `/tasks/in-progress.md` (priority-coded P0/P1/P2).
- Focus areas:
  - Nonprofit formation (legal, board, EIN, tax exemption)
  - Operational accounts setup (Gmail, GitHub, AI tools, banking, etc.)
- Tasks are **staff-led**, no volunteers.

### 🌐 Alpha Development & Hosting Setup
- **GitHub Repository**: Serves as the single source of truth and primary database. All content is stored in markdown files here.
- **Vercel Project**: Connected to the GitHub repo. Automatically deploys the contents and serves the placeholder `index.html` as a temporary frontend.
- **AI Collaborative Development (This GPT)**: The engine for making changes. Follows the workflow in this document to update the GitHub repository (via text exports/plans).
- **Protocol**: The database structure is developed here, then integrated into the GitHub repo. Vercel provides a public-facing view.
