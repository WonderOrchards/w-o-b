# Workflows — Wonder Orchards (alpha-v2.2)

## Overview
Our workflows are modeled after the life of a tree, reflecting patience, steady growth, and abundance.  

---

## Tree Roadmap Stages
- **Seeds:** Vision, planting, and cuttings  
- **Roots:** Foundation and values  
- **Trunk:** Structure and stability  
- **Branches:** Programs and projects  
- **Leaves:** People and daily work  
- **Flowers:** Ideas and initiatives  
- **Fruit:** Results and impact  
- **Cycles & Seasons:** Pacing and patience  

---

## Active Task Workflow
- Tasks tracked in [`/tasks/in-progress.md`](/tasks/in-progress.md)  
- Priority coded: **P0 / P1 / P2**  
- Focus areas:  
  - Nonprofit formation (legal, board, EIN, tax exemption)  
  - Operational accounts setup (Gmail, GitHub, AI tools, banking, etc.)  
- All tasks are **staff-led** (no volunteers).  

---

## Alpha Development & Hosting Setup
- **GitHub Repository:**  
  Serves as the single source of truth and primary database.  
  All content is stored in Markdown files here.  

- **Vercel Project:**  
  Connected to the GitHub repository.  
  Automatically deploys the contents and serves the placeholder `index.html` as a temporary frontend.  

- **AI Collaborative Development (This GPT):**  
  Acts as the engine for making changes.  
  Follows the workflow in this document to update the GitHub repository (via text exports/plans).  

- **Protocol:**  
  The database structure is developed here, then integrated into the GitHub repository.  
  Vercel provides a public-facing view.
