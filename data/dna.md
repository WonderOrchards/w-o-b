# DNA - Wonder Orchards (alpha-v2.3)

## Essence / Identity

Wonder Orchards is a staff-led nonprofit modeled after the nature of trees:

patient, steady, resilient, generous, and abundant.

We cultivate conditions in which fruit trees and people can thrive together.

Our work is practical, beautiful, playful, useful, and rooted in long-term abundance.

---

## The Orchard Within

The external orchard begins with an internal one.

We seek to cultivate the qualities we hope to encounter in the world:

- **Generosity:** Give freely and create conditions in which others can thrive.
- **Patience:** Move with seasons, allowing growth, rest, learning, and fruiting to occur in their proper time.
- **Care:** Do things thoughtfully, tending both small details and long horizons.
- **Beauty:** Treat beauty, wonder, and craftsmanship as meaningful forms of abundance.
- **Abundance:** Favor systems that create more life, more fruit, more possibility, and more opportunity to share.
- **Wonder:** Preserve a sense of curiosity and delight in living things and in what becomes possible.

---

## Operational Principles

- **Staff-led:** Wonder Orchards is built around paid contributors rather than relying on volunteer labor as its operating foundation.
- **Steady growth:** Grow deliberately and sustainably rather than rushing for scale.
- **Organic development:** Allow successful ideas and systems to propagate naturally.
- **Quality:** Build things that are useful, durable, beautiful, and worthy of care.
- **Simplicity:** Prefer simple systems that remain understandable and adaptable.
- **Participation:** Create places and opportunities where people can encounter, enjoy, learn from, and contribute to abundance.
- **Propagation:** When something works, make it easier to reproduce.
- **Generosity:** Measure success partly by what becomes available to others.

---

## Relationship With Trees

Trees are more than the subject of our work. They are a model for how we work.

A tree:

- begins small;
- establishes roots before reaching outward;
- grows through seasons;
- adapts to its environment;
- produces more than it consumes;
- creates habitat around itself;
- bears fruit;
- reproduces;
- and continues beyond the individual moment.

Wonder Orchards seeks to build systems with similar qualities.

---

## What We Value

We value:

- living systems over rigid systems;
- long-term health over short-term appearance;
- abundance over scarcity;
- participation over exclusion;
- propagation over unnecessary reinvention;
- beauty alongside usefulness;
- patience alongside action;
- wonder alongside practicality.

## Role of This Database

This database is the evolving institutional memory of Wonder Orchards.

It captures:

- vision;
- identity;
- workflows;
- decisions;
- experiments;
- tasks;
- observations;
- projects;
- and lessons learned.

The database itself should remain capable of evolving as the organization discovers what it needs.

Its structure is a tool for preserving and propagating knowledge, not a constraint on what Wonder Orchards may become.

Wonder Orchards draws inspiration from:

- fruit trees in public spaces;
- neighborhood orchards;
- household plantings;
- nurseries;
- propagation;
- children discovering fruit;
- people gathering beneath trees;
- seasonal cycles;
- and the simple wonder of something living producing more life and giving it away.
