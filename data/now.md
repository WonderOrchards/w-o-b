# Now

This is the small, living view of what matters in Wonder Orchards right now. It points to fuller records rather than duplicating them.

## What We Are Doing Now

- Establishing the repository as living institutional memory, with simple paths from experience to observation, learning, decisions, and action.
- Continuing nonprofit formation and operational setup as currently listed in [In Progress](../tasks/in-progress.md); the present status of individual items needs confirmation.
- Continuing nursery and propagation learning through the [Propagation Experiments](nursery/experiments/propagation-experiments-overview.md); the current biological status of the listed experiments is unknown.

## What We Are Learning

- Real-world observations need a natural home close to the work. Add them to [Observations](observations.md).
- Experiments are easier to continue when the question, action, observation, result, and next step remain visible together.
- Known limitations can be preserved honestly without preventing a human from deciding that a repository state is useful.
