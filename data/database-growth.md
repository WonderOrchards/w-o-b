# Database Growth — Wonder Orchards

This file tracks **gaps, missing information, and prompts** to guide further building of the database.  

---

## 🌱 Identity & DNA
- What is Wonder Orchards’ **origin story**?  
- How will staff roles and responsibilities be defined?  
- What **principles or practices** distinguish us from other nonprofits?  

## 🌱 Vision
- What does success look like in **5 years**? In **20 years**?  
- How do orchards integrate into **urban planning** or **rural communities**?  
- What is the **educational role** (schools, children, community workshops)?  

## 🌱 Workflows
- How will **projects be approved** (Seeds → Roots → Trunk → etc.)?  
- What are examples of **pilot projects** (first Seeds)?  
- What happens when a project fails — does it get logged, composted, or retried?  

## 🌱 Tasks & Operations
- Which **operational accounts** still need to be created?  
- What are the **priority legal filings** (timelines, costs)?  
- How will financial management (accounting, payroll, transparency) be tracked?  

## 🌱 Logs & Decisions
- Should logs be written **daily**, **weekly**, or only at milestones?  
- How formal should decision records be (brief notes vs. structured templates)?  

## 🌱 Expansion
- What other **docs** should be added (e.g., partnerships, funding models)?  
- Do we need a `/policies/` folder (HR, ethics, community guidelines)?  
- How should **nursery planning** be documented (site layouts, species lists)?

📌 Use this file as a **living checklist of missing info and questions**. Update as answers emerge, then migrate resolved items into proper docs.
