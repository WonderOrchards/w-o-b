# Decisions

This is the append-friendly record of consequential human decisions that should remain understandable over time.

Only a decision actually made by an accountable human belongs here. The Steward may preserve, relate, and surface a decision, but must not manufacture approval or turn a proposal into a decision.

## Add a Decision

Add the newest entry at the top of the log:

```markdown
## YYYY-MM-DD — Question

- **Decision:**
- **Why:**
- **Affected things:** [Documents, projects, experiments, or tasks](path)
- **Decided by:**
- **Date:** YYYY-MM-DD
```

Use ordinary Markdown links to connect the decision to relevant observations, experiments, tasks, or organizational documents. If the human did not provide reasoning, record `Not recorded` rather than inventing it.
