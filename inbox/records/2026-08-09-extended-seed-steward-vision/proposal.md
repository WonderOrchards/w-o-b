## Proposed Outcome

If a human wants Wonder Orchards to retain this farther horizon, extend only the existing “Stewardship Intelligence / Seed Steward” exploration in [`data/database-growth.md`](../../../data/database-growth.md) with a clearly labeled long-horizon scenario.

The addition should preserve the possibility of a modular, land-scale steward that could one day sense and tend acreage and help create food, sustainability, beauty, habitat, and human spaces. It must also state that land development, gantries, wells, dwellings, city-building, physical action, and self-expansion are speculative imagery—not adopted programs, specifications, capabilities, commitments, or authorized work.

The scenario should link to this intake case and retain explicit questions about mission alignment, multi-species well-being, human authority, consent, ecology, safety, resource limits, feasibility, and conditions for stopping or pruning expansion.

## Contradictions and Open Questions

If interpreted as present authority, the invitation for the Steward to build its brain and expansion infrastructure conflicts with its documented human-bounded repository role. The proposal preserves that distinction explicitly.

Human judgment is needed on:

- whether a land-scale or city-building horizon belongs within Wonder Orchards' mission at all;
- whether the horizon should be preserved in authoritative backlog knowledge or remain only inbox provenance;
- whose well-being, rights, and consent govern development choices;
- how ecological abundance would be distinguished from extraction, optimization, or unwanted control;
- what limits would constrain land, energy, water, materials, data, computation, replication, and physical reach;
- which decisions must always remain human or public decisions;
- how any system could be stopped, inspected, repaired, pruned, or retired; and
- what much smaller, reversible observation would need to succeed before physical experimentation could even be proposed.

## Authority Review

A human may approve, revise, defer, or reject this proposal. Approval would authorize only a bounded addition to `data/database-growth.md`.

It would not authorize:

- treating the extended vision as organizational identity, mission, strategy, or commitment;

- self-modification, autonomous expansion, replication, or resource acquisition; or
- any later step without separate evidence and human authorization.
