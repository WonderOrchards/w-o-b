## Source Summary

The source describes Wonder Orchards as developing “Stewardship Intelligence”: intelligent stewards intended to help existing abundance become visible, tangible, and shareable. It gives them the compass “Cultivate conditions for wonder” and describes a developmental arc from attention through sensing, memory, understanding, imagination, participation, stewardship, and growth.

The source identifies a first possible manifestation called the “Seed Steward,” described as a small embodied intelligence that arrives at a place, develops sensing capacity, spends substantial time learning the place, begins participating carefully, and eventually helps construct and expand its capacity to steward. It explicitly calls itself a seed rather than a specification and asks to be observed, related, interpreted, questioned, and potentially developed over time.

## Explicit Claims

The source explicitly states that:

- Wonder Orchards is developing “Stewardship Intelligence.”
- Its purpose is to help abundance already present become visible, tangible, and shareable.
- Its compass is “Cultivate conditions for wonder.”
- Its proposed developmental arc is `Attention → Sensing → Memory → Understanding → Imagination → Participation → Stewardship → Growth`.
- The first manifestation is proposed as a small embodied “Seed Steward,” rather than a large gantry.
- The Seed Steward would learn a place before carefully participating and later help expand its stewardship capacity.
- The material is a seed, not a specification.
- Placement in the inbox is intended to permit observation, relationship, interpretation, questioning, and possible later development.

These are source statements. Their presence does not establish that Wonder Orchards has adopted this direction or authorized any implementation.

## Steward Inferences

- **Inference:** “Embodied intelligence” likely points beyond the current repository Steward toward a physical or place-sensing system, but the source does not define its hardware, software, autonomy, location, or timescale.
- **Inference:** The developmental arc resembles a learning loop in which observation and memory precede action; this may be compatible with the repository's living learning cycle.
- **Inference:** Helping construct or expand its own capacity could imply self-modification, automation, or physical construction. Those meanings are not specified and would require explicit safety, governance, and human-authority boundaries.
- **Inference:** The concept could become a research direction, educational project, orchard tool, artistic expression, or organizational program. The source does not choose among these possibilities.

## Unknowns

- Who authored the seed and when it was written.
- Whether “Wonder Orchards is developing” expresses an approved present program, an aspiration, or only the source author's framing.
- What “intelligence,” “embodied,” “nervous system,” “participation,” and “stewardship” mean operationally.
- Whether the Seed Steward would observe only, recommend human action, act digitally, act physically, or combine these roles.
- What environmental, human, privacy, security, safety, legal, financial, and governance constraints would apply.
- Whether the concept advances the fruit-tree mission directly enough to belong in organizational Vision or instead remains exploratory research.
- What evidence, need, resources, and success criteria would justify moving from a seed to a specification or experiment.
- Whether “help construct and expand its own capacity” is metaphorical, human-directed, or intended to describe autonomous self-expansion.

## Institutional Significance

The genuinely new organizational possibility is not merely another repository feature. It is the idea that Wonder Orchards might cultivate a place-based, embodied intelligence according to orchard values and a staged learning arc. If adopted, it could eventually affect organizational direction, program design, technology, safety, governance, resources, public claims, and the boundary between recommendation and action.

The smallest presently supportable institutional step is to preserve the concept as an explicit exploration question. DNA, Vision, Workflows, the Steward agent, Steward version history, frontend, and active capabilities should remain unchanged unless later evidence and human decisions justify them.

## Authority and Risk

Human review is required before the source becomes an organizational direction, program, specification, experiment, public claim, or Steward capability. The source itself does not authorize those changes.

Risk is presently conceptual rather than operational because nothing has been activated. Risk would rise materially if future work introduced sensing of people or places, physical actuation, autonomous decisions, self-modification, construction, data collection, or public claims that Wonder Orchards is developing such a system.
