# Steward Interpretation

## Source Summary

Summarize the source faithfully without adding facts.

## Explicit Claims

List what the source explicitly states.

## Steward Inferences

Label each inference clearly.

## Unknowns

Record missing dates, authority, context, evidence, or definitions.

## Classification

Identify material types and candidate destinations.

## Reconciliation

For each relevant claim, identify the existing document and relationship: `confirms`, `extends`, `clarifies`, `duplicates`, `contradicts`, `may-supersede`, `historical-only`, `unrelated`, or `unresolved`.

## Institutional Significance

Explain why the material may matter and what should remain unchanged.

## Authority and Risk

Identify required human review. This interpretation is analysis, not a decision.
