Welcome to the particle-backed page

This demo places a Three.js particle field in the background and lets the camera orbit while you scroll. Use the slider in the header to control how much rotation happens per full-page scroll.

About

Camera orbit is mapped to the scroll progress across the document. Elements with the data-speed attribute will subtly parallax to create a layered feel — they live in the DOM (foreground) while the particle-field is rendered in WebGL behind everything.

More content

Keep scrolling. The camera smoothly interpolates to its target orbit so motion feels natural and pleasant. The particle field is intentionally light-weight but scalable — tweak PARTICLE_COUNT in the script if you want more or fewer points.

Final section

That's it — a simple single-file demo. Save this HTML and open in a modern browser. Slider constraints let you slow things down to zero or crank up rotation-per-scroll for dramatic effect.
